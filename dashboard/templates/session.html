What the Regression Values Mean

MSE: Mean Squared Error. It calculates the average squared difference between actual salary and predicted salary. Lower MSE means smaller prediction errors.

RMSE: Root Mean Squared Error. It is the square root of MSE and is expressed in the same unit as salary.

MAE: Mean Absolute Error. It gives the average absolute difference between actual and predicted salary.

R²: Coefficient of determination. It indicates how much of the variation in salary is explained by the model.
